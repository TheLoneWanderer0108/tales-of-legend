# Research notes

These are **study guides, not tutorials**. Each one tells you what to learn, what questions
you must be able to answer, and how to prove to yourself that you understand it. None of them
contain finished implementations, because the implementation is your job and the whole point.

## How to use one

1. Read the sections the day asks for. Budget 20-45 minutes and do not exceed it - these are
   primers, not textbooks.
2. Answer the "questions to answer" out loud or in writing **before** you code. If you cannot
   answer one, that is the thing to go and read about.
3. Do the exercises. They are small and they are where the understanding actually happens.
4. Come back and check the done-criteria after building the system.

If a note contradicts what you discover in practice, trust your practice and correct the note.
These files are yours to edit.
